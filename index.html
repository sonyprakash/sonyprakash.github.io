About Me

Hello! I'm Soni Prakash, a Technical Architect specializing in Red Hat OpenShift, Azure Red Hat OpenShift (ARO), Kubernetes, Platform Engineering, and enterprise cloud-native platforms.

My experience spans architecture, deployment, operations, upgrades, and troubleshooting of OpenShift environments running across Bare Metal, virtualized infrastructure, and public cloud platforms.

I regularly work on OpenShift cluster lifecycle management, upgrade planning, operator lifecycle management, infrastructure architecture, platform reliability, networking, DNS, observability, logging, security, and production incident investigations.

Over the years, I have helped architect, deploy, operate, and troubleshoot enterprise OpenShift platforms supporting business-critical applications and large-scale containerized environments.

Through this blog, I share real-world troubleshooting scenarios, Root Cause Analysis (RCA), architecture insights, upgrade experiences, best practices, and lessons learned from managing OpenShift in production.

I am also building OpenShift Upgrade Advisor, a platform focused on identifying upgrade blockers, deprecated APIs, operator risks, and cluster readiness issues before OpenShift upgrades.
